Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This policy explains how the Cosmic Galaxy Discord bot ("the Bot", "we") collects, uses, and protects your data when you use the Bot in any Discord server. By using the Bot, you agree to the practices described here.

The Bot is not directed at children under the age of 13, in line with Discord's own Terms of Service. If we learn that someone under this age is using the Bot, we will delete their data immediately.

1. Data We Collect

  • User ID and username — when you use any Bot command, your ID and username are recorded so we can link your data (balance, warnings) to you.
  • Guild ID — to store each server's settings separately (language, welcome messages, tickets, etc.).
  • Economy data — your "Galaxies" balance (global) and "Stardust" balance (per-server), and the timestamp of your last daily reward.
  • Warning history — if a server admin warns you through the Bot, the reason, date, and moderator are stored.
  • Server settings — channel and role IDs linked to welcome/leave messages, auto-roles, and the ticket system, as configured by each server's admins.

The Bot does not read or store your regular messages. We only process messages that start with a Bot command (e.g. !daily), and the content of warning reasons or announcements that an admin writes using dedicated commands for that purpose.

2. How We Use the Data

  • Operating the Bot's features (economy, moderation, tickets, welcome messages).
  • Persisting each server's settings so they aren't lost when the Bot restarts.
  • Improving Bot stability and diagnosing technical issues.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Your data is stored on the Bot's hosting servers and a MongoDB database. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but as with any online service, no one can guarantee absolute 100% security.

4. Sharing Data With Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any marketing party. Data is only shared in the following cases:

  • With our hosting and database providers, only to the extent needed to run the Bot.
  • If required by a legitimate legal authority under a valid court order.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as the Bot remains in the server and as long as you use it. If an admin removes the Bot from a server, that server's settings are deleted. Your personal account data (like your Galaxies balance) remains linked to your account ID across every server until you request its deletion.

6. Your Rights

  • Right to access — you may request a copy of the data we store about you.
  • Right to deletion — you may request that your data be permanently deleted from the database.
  • Right to correction — if any data is inaccurate, you may request a correction.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: contact.pulsegrid@gmail.com — or our support server: discord.gg/R6AqTeYZFJ

7. Cookies

The Bot itself (inside Discord) does not use any cookies. If we launch a web dashboard with login in the future, this section will be updated in detail at that time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any significant change will be announced on our official support server and/or via a Bot message. Continuing to use the Bot after an update means you accept the new version.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: contact.pulsegrid@gmail.com / discord.gg/R6AqTeYZFJ
